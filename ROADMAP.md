# Roadmap

Future modules, not included in this release:

- Online storefront and pickup orders.
- Subscription plans, billing, quotas and tenant lifecycle administration.
- Product images, barcode labels and camera scanning.
- Fractional units, bundles and variants.
- Purchase-order drafts, supplier balances and multi-line purchase invoices.
- Partial returns, exchanges and payment reconciliation.
- Cost accounting, expenses and financial ledgers.
- Batch/expiry and serial-number tracking.
- Merchant-approved card/wallet APIs and dynamic QR verification.
- Email delivery, password reset, MFA and distributed rate limiting.
- Burmese interface translations.
- PostgreSQL profiles and multi-cashier load testing.
- Direct receipt-printer and cash-drawer integrations.

Add modules with permissions, migrations and workflow tests.
